## The wedge — how to win

- **Fix the money model** (207 gripes — the dominant complaint): win with a genuinely useful free tier or a one-time unlock, not another subscription/paywall-after-2-entries.
- **Ship ad-free** (49 gripes): ads are a repeated complaint here, and 'no ads' is itself a selling point users praise.
- **Keep what they love:** love, best, free, easy, fast — don't out-feature these into complexity.
