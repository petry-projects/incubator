## The wedge — how to win

- **Fix the money model** (137 gripes — the dominant complaint): win with a genuinely useful free tier or a one-time unlock, not another subscription/paywall-after-2-entries.
- **Be the simple, un-bloated alternative** (47 gripes): users say the incumbents got worse / raised prices / removed basics — restore the clean original experience.
- **Ship ad-free** (40 gripes): ads are a repeated complaint here, and 'no ads' is itself a selling point users praise.
- **Make reliability the pitch** (34 gripes): recurring crashes / broken widgets / lost-data complaints — rock-solid sync + export/backup is a real differentiator.
- **Keep what they love:** love, best, free, easy, simple — don't out-feature these into complexity.
